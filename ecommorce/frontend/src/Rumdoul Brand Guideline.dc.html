01 · The brand

Who Rumdoul is

Rumdoul is a Phnom Penh coffee shop named after Cambodia’s national flower. It sells Khmer coffee and Khmer sweets to people who are in a hurry in the morning and in no hurry at all in the afternoon. The brand is warm, loud and a little bit cheeky — never precious about coffee.

We are

Playful, generous, quick, proudly local. We use short sentences, real numbers and the occasional joke at our own expense.

We are not

Third-wave-serious, minimal-beige, or full of tasting notes nobody asked for. No “artisanal journey”. No whispering.

Voice & tone

Say this Not this
Order in two taps.Begin your seamless ordering journey.
Fried at six, gone by eleven.Freshly prepared daily by our team.
Nothing brewing yet.Your cart is currently empty.
Cancel any time, no hard feelings.Subject to our cancellation policy.
“

Rule of thumb: if a barista wouldn’t say it across the counter, it doesn’t go on the site.

02 · Logo

The mark & lockup

The mark is a four-petal abstraction of the rumdoul flower with a pink centre, always inside a yellow disc with a 3px ink outline. The wordmark is Baloo 2 ExtraBold at −0.02em tracking.

05 · Layout

Spacing & grid

Container

width: min(94vw, 1320px), centred. Section rhythm is clamp(56px, 7vw, 104px) top and bottom.

No fixed widths anywhere. Every grid is auto-fit / minmax() so it reflows without media queries.

Spacing scale

5 · hairline gap 10 · inside a pill 20 · card padding 32 · grid gutter 56 · block spacing 104 · section padding

Breakpoints

< 760px · Phone

1 column everywhere. Nav collapses to a pill drawer. Spotlight cards stack and fade up — no pinning.

760–1023px · Tablet

2-up grids. Menu filter becomes a horizontal bar above the grid. Card stack pins in a 2×2 fan.

1024px+ · Desktop

Full nav, sidebar filter, 3–4-up grids, 4-across pinned card fan, zigzag spine visible at 1280px+.

Corner & border language

Radii step 14 / 18 / 22 / 26 / 30 / 999px. Every surface carries a 2–3px Ink border and a hard offset shadow (0 8px 0 -1px rgba(67,38,60,.16)) — never a soft blur at rest. Blur is reserved for hover lift.

07 · Motion

Motion principles

All motion is GSAP with ScrollTrigger. The rule that governs everything: the page must be complete and correct with zero animation frames. Motion is enhancement, never the thing that makes content appear.

Card deck → fan

The spotlight section pins for ~1.1 viewport heights. Four cards start as a squared-up deck and scrub out into their grid positions with alternating rotation. Fallback: the plain grid.

Ribbon draw

Ink stroke then yellow stroke draw via stroke-dashoffset, scrubbed across the band's viewport crossing; the path text fades in at 50% and then marquees forever.

Hover

One paused timeline per card, played forward on enter and reversed on leave. Lift −10 to −12px, photo scale 1.05–1.10, order cue slides up from below the fold of the card.

Feedback

Adding to the bag pops the counter (scale 1.45, yoyo) and slides an ink toast up from the bottom for 2.1s. Payment success springs the tick in with back.out(2.2).
